How I work with AI

I used to need a whole team to try out an idea. Now I plan the work, hand it to a crew of AI agents, and spend my own time on what they can't do: deciding what matters and checking what they build. Same QA instincts, much bigger reach.

  1. 01 I set the direction Goal, plan and guardrails, written down so the agents can follow them.
  2. 02 Agents do the legwork Specialised subagents with tools (MCP) and reusable skills, often several in parallel.
  3. 03 I review and ship Tests, code review and the final call on quality. That part stays mine.
